MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA-2026-007)

This Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2026 between ACME Supply Co
("Disclosing Party") and Northwind Trading LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. Purpose. The parties wish to explore a supply arrangement for warehouse
   fittings and will disclose confidential information for that purpose.

2. Confidential Information. Includes pricing, forecasts, drawings, and any
   material marked confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as such.

3. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall protect the information, limit access
   to employees with a need to know, and not disclose it to third parties.

4. Term. This Agreement remains in effect for two years. Either party may
   terminate it on thirty days written notice; confidentiality obligations
   survive termination for three years.

5. Governing law. The laws of the State of Oregon govern this Agreement.

Signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
ACME Supply Co — J. Reyes, VP Operations
Northwind Trading LLC — P. Osei, Director of Procurement
